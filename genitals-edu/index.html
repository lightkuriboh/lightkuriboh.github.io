TissueWeek 7FemaleMale
1. SRY on the Y chromosome (Yp11.2) triggers testis determination (Sertoli & Leydig cells).
2. Fetal Testosterone. Secreted by Leydig cells beginning Week 8.
3. 5α-reductase type 2 converts testosterone locally to dihydrotestosterone (DHT).
4. Androgen receptor signalling. Drives phallic elongation, ventral fold fusion (penile raphe/spongy urethra), and scrotal swelling fusion.
5. Absence of DHT (46,XX). Yields default female morphology: glans clitoridis, unfused labia minora, labia majora, and patent vestibule.
Pubertal Steroid Surge. Hypothalamic GnRH stimulates pituitary LH/FSH. In 46,XX, ovarian estrogens expand labial adipose, clitoral vascularity, and vaginal lubrication. In 46,XY, testicular testosterone/DHT expands penile corpora, glans volume, and scrotal dartos rugae.
Parasympathetic Erection & Vasocongestion (S2–S4). Pelvic splanchnic nerves release Nitric Oxide (NO) → soluble guanylyl cyclase → cGMP → smooth muscle relaxation of helicine arterioles.
• Male: Cavernosal spaces engorge; subtunical venules compress against tunica albuginea (veno-occlusive mechanism), producing rigid erection (+55% length, +35% girth). Scrotum contracts and ascends.
• Female: Clitoral corpora and vestibular bulbs engorge; labia minora swell, blush, and part laterally to reveal the vaginal introitus; labia majora flatten slightly; epithelial transudation produces lubrication.
Sympathetic Resolution (T11–L2). Norepinephrine → vasoconstriction → PDE5 degrades cGMP → detumescence back to resting flaccid state.
Hypospadias
Incomplete ventral fusion of urethral folds leaves the urethral meatus proximal on the ventral shaft.
Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome (CAIS)
46,XY individuals with non-functional AR exhibit female-typical external anatomy despite intra-abdominal testes.
5α-Reductase Type 2 Deficiency
Impaired conversion of T to DHT causes under-virilized neonatal genitalia, often with significant virilization at puberty under high testosterone.
Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (46,XX)
Adrenal androgen excess virilizes female external genitalia (clitoromegaly and partial labioscrotal fusion).
